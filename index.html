      Privacy Policy

      Last updated: April 2026

      Data We Collect

      Nexus operates entirely within your browser. All data — tasks, history, accounts, settings — is stored locally using localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server.

      One-Time Login

      When you log in, your username is saved to this device's localStorage so you stay logged in automatically. You can sign out manually anytime from Settings.

      Uploaded Files

      Custom ringtones and wallpapers you upload are stored as data URLs in localStorage. They never leave your device.

      Third-Party Services

      Fonts from Google Fonts, icons from Font Awesome CDN, and preset images from Unsplash may log your IP when loaded.

      Your Rights

      Clear all data anytime via your browser's localStorage settings. No remote data exists.
